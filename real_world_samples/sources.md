**Note on the NOAA links:** the URLs above are collection search queries rather
than permalinks to each individual image. All NOAA imagery is public domain, so
reuse is unrestricted either way; the search terms are recorded so the images
can be located again.

**Note on the two own photos:** used deliberately as out-of-domain
failure-oriented cases. Neither model has a "none of the above" class, so a
human subject forces it to either stay silent or produce a confident wrong
label — which is exactly the behaviour discussed in the failure analysis.
